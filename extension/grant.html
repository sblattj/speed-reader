Read inside embedded frames

Some pages show their text inside an embedded frame (an iframe) served from a different site, like document viewers, previews, and course players. Speed Reader can only see that text with your permission.

Speed Reader still only runs when you press its button, shortcut, or menu item, and still sends nothing anywhere. This just lets it read the frames on the page you invoke it on. You can turn it off here any time.
